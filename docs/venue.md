## 2026 Congress Venue

University of Milan - La Statale\
Via Festa del Perdono 7\
20122 Milano (MI), Italy\
[www.unimi.it](https://www.unimi.it)

The University of Milan, known as La Statale, is one of Italy's most prestigious universities, located in the heart of Milan. The historic main campus at Via Festa del Perdono 7 offers a beautiful setting combining Renaissance architecture with modern academic facilities.
